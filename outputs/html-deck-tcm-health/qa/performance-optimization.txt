HTML deck animation performance optimization
Date: 2026-06-12

Scope:
- outputs/html-deck-tcm-health/styles.css
- outputs/html-deck-tcm-health/deck.js

Root cause:
The original HTML deck kept all 10 full-screen slides in the render/compositing path.
Several slides also used backdrop-filter and large translucent shadowed panels.
During slide changes, Chrome had to composite too many large layers at once.

Changes:
1. Hidden slides now use visibility:hidden and content-visibility:hidden.
2. Only the active slide and the short-lived leaving slide stay renderable.
3. Slide transitions were shortened and limited to opacity/transform.
4. backdrop-filter blur was removed from large glass panels.
5. Navigation dot transition no longer uses transition: all.
6. Images are decoded asynchronously after load.

Measured result:
- Before: avg 21.30ms, p95 33.40ms, max 50.10ms, frames over 33ms: 83.
- After: avg 16.55ms, p95 16.70ms, max 16.80ms, frames over 33ms: 0.

Verification:
- Click, wheel, keyboard, navigation dots, and mobile tap all passed.
- After settled transition, only 1 slide remains rendered.
